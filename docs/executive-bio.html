Full Bio (~200 words · for detailed program notes)

Shubhojit Chowdhury is an Enterprise AI, Digital Experience & Commerce Architect currently serving Fortune 500 clients through IBM Consulting. With 19+ years of enterprise technology experience, he designs and delivers governed AI systems, intelligent content supply chains, composable-commerce ecosystems, and cloud-native enterprise platforms across the full transformation lifecycle — from strategy through to production.

His architecture practice spans IBM watsonx agentic AI, Adobe Experience Cloud (AEM, AEP/RTCDP, Workfront, Fusion, Target), enterprise commerce platforms (IBM WebSphere Commerce, SAP Hybris), composable-commerce reference architecture, and enterprise integration (middleware, API gateways, event-driven architecture, BFF patterns). He has led enterprise programs for clients in telecom, media & advertising, grocery retail, and travel across the United States.

Shubhojit is an Adobe Certified Master with 25+ Credly credentials, an active IEEE member, and President of Sreeshti — a Bengali cultural nonprofit in New Jersey. He is an invited judge for the 2026 Claro Awards for Technology Excellence, a web development volunteer with 48in48, and a member of a Community Advisory Board.
