01 / About

Who I Am

I am an Enterprise AI and Digital Transformation Architect based in Hillsborough, New Jersey, currently at IBM Consulting. Over 19 years I have delivered mission-critical technology programs for some of the largest enterprises in the United States — in telecommunications, retail, media, travel, and financial services — from strategy through production.

My expertise sits at a rare intersection: I design and govern enterprise-scale digital platforms, AI-powered systems, and intelligent content supply chains that directly drive revenue, customer experience, and operational efficiency for Fortune 500 organizations. I work across the complete transformation lifecycle — translating complex business problems into architecture decisions, leading engineering teams, integrating enterprise systems, and ensuring solutions reach production on time and at scale.

I hold the Adobe Certified Master designation — the highest level of Adobe certification — along with three additional Adobe Expert certifications and more than 25 verified Credly digital badges from Adobe and IBM. I am a member of IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, Member #102577671), the world's largest technical professional organization. I serve as a judge for the 2026 Claro Awards for Technology Excellence, recognizing innovation across the US technology industry.

Beyond my professional work, I contribute directly to American communities: as President of Sreeshti, a New Jersey community service organization, and as a volunteer Web Developer for 48in48 — a national initiative that builds websites for US nonprofits in 48 hours. I am committed to this country, to the communities I serve, and to the American technology ecosystem that I have helped strengthen for nearly two decades.
